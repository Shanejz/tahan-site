Tahan

Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

In short: Tahan keeps everything on your phone. It has no account, no servers, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn’t collect, sell or share your data.

Who we are

Tahan (“the app”) is a personal budgeting app for Android made by Shane, an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

What the app stores

Everything you enter stays on your phone:

  • your entries (expenses, income, their names, categories, notes, dates and amounts), your wallet and monthly budget;
  • holds (things you are thinking of buying and their prices);
  • loans, including the names of the people you lend to or borrow from;
  • the questions you ask the analyst and its answers;
  • your settings, such as language, currency and reminders.

This information is saved in a database on your phone that is encrypted with a key kept in your phone’s secure keystore. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing through the app. Tahan has no account or sign-in, no servers of its own, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We can’t see your entries, your numbers or anything else you put into the app. The only time we receive anything from you is if you choose to email us (see Feedback emails below).

The analyst

The analyst works on your phone. Your questions and your numbers are not sent to any server or AI service. If a future version ever offers an online feature, it will be optional, this policy will be updated first, and the app will tell you before anything leaves your phone.

Backups and moving phones

The app’s data is not included in Android or Google backups, and isn’t copied when you move to a new phone. To keep a copy, use Import & export in the app: it saves your log as a CSV file in a place you choose. That file is yours, and it is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are set up on your phone and shown by Android; nothing is sent to us. Home-screen widgets show your amounts on your home screen, where anyone who can see your screen can read them; you can remove a widget at any time.

Feedback emails

If you email us, for example with Send feedback in the app (Account > About Tahan), the message is written and sent by your own email app, and only when you send it. We receive your email address and what you write. To help us understand problems, the app adds a line with the app version, your phone model, your Android version and the app’s language; you can change or delete it before sending. We use your email only to reply and to improve Tahan, we don’t share it with anyone, and we delete it when it’s no longer needed. To ask us to delete it sooner, email tahan.support@gmail.com.

Google Play

Tahan is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information such as installs and crash reports under its own privacy policy, and it shares anonymous crash and performance statistics with developers. See Google’s privacy policy.

Links and this website

The privacy policy and the terms of use open in your phone’s browser. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, but GitHub, like any website host, may record visitors’ IP addresses (see GitHub’s privacy statement). Nothing from the app is sent when you open these pages.

Deleting your data

You are in control. Delete single entries in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling Tahan or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that deletes all of it.

Children

Tahan is not made for children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app’s update notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email tahan.support@gmail.com.
