Tahan

Think beforeyou spend.

Tahan is a money log that helps you think twice before you buy. Log what goes out, hold the things you want for a day, and see what is really left.

Closed testing on Google Play

What it does

Log in seconds

Expenses, income and money you lend or borrow, on a keypad made for it.

Hold it first

Want something? Hold it for 24 hours. Most wants fade, and Tahan counts what you didn’t spend.

Budget left, per day

See what is left this month and how much can go out each day to stay on budget.

Charts that answer

Week, month and year: where the money went, income against spending, and your net worth over time.

Ask before you buy

The analyst reads your own numbers and tells you what a purchase means for your month.

On your home screen

Widgets for budget left, holds, net worth and a one-tap quick log, with amounts hidden if you like.

Your money stays on your phone

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything is kept on your phone in an encrypted database and never sent anywhere. Read the privacy policy →
